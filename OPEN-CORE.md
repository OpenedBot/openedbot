# Open core

Status as of 2026-11-13.

## The promise

No core feature ever becomes paid. Only new things and services are sold. A
feature listed as free below stays free and stays in the AGPL core.

## Free forever

For individuals and basic teams, on their own machine or instance:

- Chat, bots, rooms and routines.
- Connectors, including the pre-registered OAuth apps.
- The "ask before acting" approval engine, tool policies and the decision
  journal.
- The local audit hash chain.
- Basic team: invitations, the owner, admin and member roles, and no member
  limit on your own instance.
- Export of your data.
- The desktop app.
- `edge-setup/` (host side) and `protocol/` (the connector protocol), under
  Apache-2.0.

## Paid

Cloud for professionals and businesses, delivered through a private plug-in
layer that is not in this repository:

- Enterprise SSO and SCIM.
- Natural-language permission rules and the automatic reviewer.
- Anchored audit: WORM storage, proof export, retention.
- Agencies managing several client companies.
- Support access.
- The hosted cloud, and relay and push at scale.
- The [commercial license](COMMERCIAL.md).

## About the openedbot.app account

An openedbot.app account (Google sign-in, or email, password and a one-time
code) is the default way to use openedbot, not a lock: the code is AGPL, and
you can run your own instance.
