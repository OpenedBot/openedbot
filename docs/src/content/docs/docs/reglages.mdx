A bot's settings are in **Manage the bot…**, from the bot's menu: they apply to that bot only. The console's **Settings** apply to your workspace and all your bots.

## AI provider and model

The AI provider makes your bots talk and charges for their answers, depending on your account with it. The provider, model and effort used by default are set in **Settings > Models**.

For a single bot, choose its model in **Manage the bot… > Model** and, when your machine allows it, its own keys in **Manage the bot… > Provider keys**.

The provider keys stay on the machine you linked. Never copy them into a conversation.

## Persona

The persona tells the bot who it is, what its mission is and how to help you. You change it in **Manage the bot… > Persona**. See [Customize your bot](/docs/bot#customize-your-bot).

## Tools and connectors

- **Manage the bot… > Skills and tools** and **Manage the bot… > MCP connectors**: what this bot can use.
- **Settings > Connectors**: the tools, MCP servers and skills your machine offers, read-only.

Turn on only what the bot needs.

## Memory

Each bot can keep its own facts from one conversation to the next. In **Manage the bot… > Memory**, you can view them, **Add** some, **Correct** them or make the bot **Forget** them. The bot does not automatically keep everything said in its conversations.

## Routines

A routine is a task a bot carries out regularly. In **Manage the bot… > Routines**, choose **New routine**, then a name, a **Schedule** and the **Message** that tells the bot what to do. You can pause a routine or turn it back on. Its results arrive in the bot's conversation.

## Actions to approve

Some actions wait for your decision. Read the request in the conversation, then choose **Allow once** or **Deny**. Each request needs its own decision. Pending requests are also gathered in **Inbox**.

A bot's **Permission mode**, chosen in its conversation, says what it can do without asking you:

- **Auto review**: an automatic reviewer answers, and asks you when it hesitates.
- **Ask**: every write or risky command is put to you.
- **Plan mode**: the bot reads and plans; writes are blocked.
- **Full access**: writes run without asking; the baseline protections still apply.

A bot's own rules are in **Manage the bot… > Approval rules**. The log of your decisions is in **Settings > Decisions**, and the rules applied to the whole workspace in **Settings > Policies**.

## Your account and your workspace

- **Settings > My account**: your **Username** (the one your bots use), the **Sign-in** section (address, Google, password) and the **Members** of your workspace. The display name comes from your sign-in provider and is not changed here.
- **Settings > Appearance and language**: the theme and the language, saved in this browser.
- **Settings > Security**: the current session, the connected devices and the password.
