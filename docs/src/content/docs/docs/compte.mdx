Open [openedbot.app](https://openedbot.app).

## Create your account

You can sign up with Google or with your email address. With email, enter the six-digit code sent to your inbox: no password is required. The same form creates your account and signs you back in.

The code is valid for ten minutes and allows five attempts. If the message is delayed, check your spam folder. After one minute, you can request a new code; use the most recent one. If the code expires or you use all five attempts, request another.

You can link your Google account later in **Settings > My account**, section **Sign-in**.

## Follow the welcome tour

After you sign up, the console guides you through two steps:

1. **Workspace**: the name of your organization, where your bots and conversations will live, then your **Username**, the one your bots will use to address you (2 to 32 characters: letters, digits and hyphens).
2. **Setup**: your bots' machine, the **AI provider** and the **Model**. A short try checks the model in the background, and you land in the app right away. The details for the machine are in [Link a machine](/docs/machine).

You can skip the **Setup** step with **Set up later**, then link the machine later from **Settings > Edge**.

The tour does not create a bot: you create the first one afterwards in the app, with **New bot**. See [Create a bot](/docs/bot).
