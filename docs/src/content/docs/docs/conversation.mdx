Choose a bot in the console to open its conversation. There you can ask a question, give an instruction or pick up an earlier exchange.

## Send a message

Type in the field at the bottom of the conversation.

- On a computer, **Enter** sends the message and **Shift + Enter** adds a new line.

## Read the answers

The bot can answer with text, links, lists or code.

## Receive an image or a PDF

Ask the bot to make an image or a PDF (a diagram, a one-image summary, a report): the file arrives under its answer, as an attachment.

- Click an image to open it large, then **Download the file** to keep it. **Esc** or a click outside closes it.
- A PDF or another document opens in the reading panel, next to the conversation.

If the answer shows only the file's name, in code, the bot did not save it where openedbot can read it. Ask again: the bot knows it must save it in its work folder. An image is at most 1 MB, and a message carries at most 5 files.

If the bot is busy, wait for its answer to finish or stop it with the **Stop generating** button. On a computer, in a single bot's conversation, **Esc** stops it too when the message field is empty.

If an action needs your approval, read the request, then choose **Allow once** or **Deny**. You find your decisions in **Settings > Decisions**. See [Actions to approve](/docs/reglages#actions-to-approve).

## Talk to several bots in a group

A group is a conversation shared with several bots.

1. Choose **New group**.
2. Give it a name and choose its **Members**: 2 to 6 bots.
3. If you like, choose the **Default responders** and the **Answer mode**: **Everyone answers**, or **Team answer**, where the members contribute and then a single final answer is written.

To address a specific bot, mention it with @ followed by its name. An explicit @mention always takes priority over the default responders.

## Hide a conversation

Hiding removes the conversation from your usual list. It is neither archived nor deleted. You find it in **Settings > Archives**.

The bot does not answer? See [Solve a problem](/docs/depannage).
