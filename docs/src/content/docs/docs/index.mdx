openedbot lets you create AI bots tailored to your tasks. You set them up and talk to them in the console. Your bots do their work on **your** machine, not in the openedbot cloud. Once that machine is linked, your bots can answer you.

Your bots run on [Hermes](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent), the reference agent that `edge-setup` installs. Other agents or gateways can also connect to openedbot through its public, versioned connector protocol.

## Your path

1. [Create an account and your workspace](/docs/compte).
2. [Link a machine](/docs/machine).
3. [Create your first bot](/docs/bot) with **New bot**.
4. [Start a conversation](/docs/conversation).

You can start setting up a bot before you link a machine. It will answer once the machine is ready.
