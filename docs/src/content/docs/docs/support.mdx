Support is provided by a person from the openedbot team. Your message is not sent to a bot.

## From the console

In the app, choose **Contact support** at the top of the screen: it opens the Support page. There you can **Attach a screenshot** and use lists, links or code.

Outside the app, for example on the sign-in page or during the welcome tour, a help bubble at the bottom of the screen opens support. If you are not signed in yet, it asks for an email address so the team can reply to you.

## Help us understand

Describe:

- what you were trying to do;
- what you saw, copying the text of the error message if you can;
- whether the problem is about your account, your machine or a bot;
- since when it has been happening.

## Follow the conversation

The conversation stays on the Support page, with the team's answer. You can pick it up later, including from another device with the same account; **My conversations** lists your exchanges. When the team has closed a conversation, write in it to pick it up again, or open a **New conversation**.

Do not send your password, your provider keys or a ticket.
