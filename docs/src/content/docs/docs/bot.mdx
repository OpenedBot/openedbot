A bot is an assistant you set up to help you with a task. Each bot has its own name, persona and model.

## Create a bot

1. In the console, choose **New bot**.
2. Start from a ready-made template, or from a blank page.
3. Give it a name, then create it. A name is enough: everything else is set later in **Manage the bot…**, from the bot's menu.

The bot's AI model is chosen in **Manage the bot… > Model**. Without a model of its own, it follows your machine's, set in **Settings > Models**. The AI provider may charge for answers, depending on your account with it.

You can create a bot before you link a machine. It will wait for the machine to be linked before it works.

To start from an existing bot, choose **Duplicate…** in its menu. The persona, face, model and preferences carry over; its approval rules start again at "ask before acting".

## Import existing bots

If your machine already holds bots, open **Settings > Import agents** and pick the ones you need. Imported routines stay paused until you turn them on.

## Customize your bot

In **Manage the bot…**:

- **Persona**: who the bot is, its mission and how it helps you. Give it precise instructions that are easy to follow.
- **Skills and tools**: what the bot can use.
- **MCP connectors**: the outside services it can plug into.

The other settings (memory, routines, approvals) are described in [Set up your bots and your account](/docs/reglages).

## Archive or delete a bot

Archiving removes the bot from your usual list without erasing its conversations. You find it in **Settings > Archives** and can restore it.

Deleting a bot sends it to the trash. Its sessions and profile are erased on your machine, but you can still restore it from **Settings > Archives**: it comes back to the list with an empty profile. The console asks you to confirm before deleting it.

To erase it for good, open **Settings > Archives**, section **Trash**, and choose **Delete permanently**. The bot, its conversations and their history are then erased, on your machine too. This deletion cannot be undone.
